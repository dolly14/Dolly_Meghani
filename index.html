NearMe for Bumble

A Proximity-Based Discovery Feature for Bumble Dating & BFF

A full product case study: market research, problem definition, PRD, Figma prototype, and structured user testing with 8 participants. NearMe helps users convert digital matches into real-world meetups — faster, safer, and with full consent control.

🔍 The Problem

Dating app users consistently struggle to convert digital matches into real-world interactions. Despite high match volumes, most conversations fail to progress to an in-person meeting — creating fatigue and churn.

  • Average of 20+ messages exchanged before a date is suggested
  • 11-day average lag from match to first date
  • Competing proximity tools (Tinder Radar, Happn) lack consent-based safety mechanisms
  • Urban users share physical spaces with compatible matches daily — and never know it

💡 The Solution

NearMe is a consent-based, proximity discovery feature for Bumble's Dating and BFF modes. Users must explicitly opt in to share their location — and can only view others who have also consented.

  • Approximate locations shown on a grid — never pinned on a map
  • Visibility is strictly reciprocal and time-limited
  • Three sub-features: Pulse (browse nearby), Moments (broadcast presence), Spark (alert when a match is nearby)
  • Freemium model: limited access for free users, unlimited for premium

Core Features
What NearMe Delivers
📡

Pulse

Browse a proximity grid of nearby users who've consented to share their location. Profiles show thumbnail, name, age, interests, and approximate distance.

✨

Moments

Broadcast your presence at a location for a time-limited session. Nearby users can discover you and send a "Say Hello" tap to initiate connection.

⚡

Spark

Get notified when an existing match enters your proximity. Reduces the 11-day avg match-to-meetup lag to under 5 days with a contextual nudge.

🔒

Consent Toggle

A prominent opt-in toggle with clear confirmation dialog. Location sharing is never on by default — fully reciprocal and auto-times out.

⚙️

Location Timeout

Users set their own sharing window (30 min, 1 hr, etc.). System auto-disables when the timer expires — no forgotten visibility.

💎

Freemium Monetization

Free users get limited proximity interactions per time window. Premium subscribers get unlimited access — creating a clear upgrade incentive.

Research Outcomes
What We Kept, What We Cut
✅ Implemented Based on Feedback
Grid-based approximate locations (not map pins) for safety and privacy
Default location timeout to prevent forgotten visibility
User-configurable radius distance setting
Thumbnail previews on Moments cards for profile context
Moments in Dating mode hides profiles of users who've expressed interest (group view for BFF only)
Max 5 users per Moments group to contain security risk
❌ Removed Based on Feedback
Shared Routes feature — raised concerns about app storing frequented locations
Location broadcast to other users — stalking and screenshot-sharing risk
Streak/gamification scoring — users felt it conflicted with dating intent
Repeated profile display after like/dislike action on Spark
